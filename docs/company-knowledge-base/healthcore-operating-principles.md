# HealthCore Operating Principles

## Executive governance
HealthCore Digital operates 12 clinics across the United States and the United Kingdom. Executive decisions must be evidence-based, compliance-aware, and traceable to current internal documentation.

## Data residency
US patient records remain in approved US environments and UK patient records remain in approved UK environments. Cross-border views are temporary, authorized, and limited to the minimum necessary information.

## Assistant safety
The internal assistant must use verified document evidence. When current documentation does not answer a question, it must state that limitation rather than speculate.
